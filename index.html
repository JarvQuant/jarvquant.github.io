FAQ

Questions traders ask before installing.

Something else? Ask in Discord

What is JarvQuant?

A desktop trading simulator and training environment for retail traders. You practise stocks, futures, forex and options against replayed historical data and synthetic markets, then review your own behaviour with the journal, Trade Autopsy and the VANTA coach.

Is real money involved?

No. You trade against historical data replayed bar by bar, or against synthetic markets from the JQIV engine. No orders are routed to a broker and no real money is at risk.

Can I practise options trading?

Yes. PANTHEON is a synthetic options market with a full chain, live Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), an implied volatility surface and an order book. Practise long calls and puts, covered calls, spreads and straddles without risking capital.

How is this different from paper trading with a broker?

Broker paper trading runs in real time, so rehearsing a setup means waiting for it to happen again. JarvQuant replays history at any speed, so you can work through hundreds of setups in an afternoon while future bars stay hidden.

Can I prepare for a prop firm challenge?

Yes. The Prop Firm Simulator reproduces evaluation rules such as profit targets, maximum daily loss and trailing or end-of-day drawdown, so you learn how you behave under those rules before paying an evaluation fee.

Is it free, and is there a trial?

The free plan includes bar-by-bar replay, data import and a journal capped at 10 entries, with no card and no time limit. A 7-day Elite trial can be started inside the app and unlocks everything, including PANTHEON and VANTA.

Does it work with MetaTrader 5?

Yes. Import your MT5 trade history through the data bridge, or load CSV and Parquet files, and analyse your real trading in Trade Autopsy and the journal next to your simulated sessions.

Which platforms are supported?

JarvQuant is a native Windows desktop app with a one-click installer. Every release is published on GitHub and scanned on VirusTotal. The interface is available in English and German.
