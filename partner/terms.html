JarvQuant Partner Terms

Version 2026-10-21

1. The program

As a JarvQuant partner you get a personal link (jarvquant.com/r/<your name>). You earn a commission on the payments of customers who sign up through it. Taking part is free and either side can end it at any time (see 8).

2. Commission

  • 15 % of each payment, 20 % once 10 of your referred customers are paying at the same time, 25 % from 25. The rate that applies is the one reached when the payment is made.
  • Commission is earned on every payment of a referred customer — the first one and every renewal — for as long as they pay. One-time purchases (e.g. Lifetime) earn once.
  • The basis is the amount actually paid, without VAT or other taxes.
  • Refunds and chargebacks cancel the commission of that payment. If it was already paid out, it is deducted from your next payouts.
  • We may change rates or tiers for future payments with 30 days' notice by e-mail. Commission already earned is never reduced by such a change.

3. Attribution

  • A customer is yours if they register after opening your link — in the same browser or network shortly after, or with the e-mail address they gave on our website after following your link — or by entering your link name in the app. The first partner wins and the attribution is permanent.
  • Ad blockers, private browsing or switching devices can prevent attribution. We do our best but can't guarantee that every visitor is recorded.

4. Payouts

  • Payouts are made through Stripe Connect. You set up your payout account with Stripe once; Stripe may ask for identity and bank details. Without a completed setup we keep your balance until it is done.
  • Commission becomes payable 30 days after the payment (refund period). We pay monthly once at least €25 are payable; smaller amounts carry over.
  • Payouts are in euros. Stripe's or your bank's conversion and transfer fees may apply.
  • All commission amounts are gross and include any VAT you may owe on them.
  • You are responsible for declaring and paying taxes on your commission.

5. Promoting JarvQuant

  • Say clearly that your link is an affiliate link (e.g. "affiliate link" or "#ad"), as the law in your country requires.
  • Only make true statements about JarvQuant. No profit promises, no "guaranteed" results, no financial advice in our name. JarvQuant is a tool for practising trading, not a signal service.
  • Not allowed: spam (unsolicited messages, comment spam), buying through your own link or referring yourself, fake or incentivised sign-ups, bidding on "JarvQuant" in paid search ads, pretending to be JarvQuant, and forcing your link on people who never clicked it.

6. Fraud

Commission from accounts or payments that break section 5 is not owed. In clear cases of fraud we can close the partner account and withhold the commission involved.

7. Data

We show you aggregated numbers (clicks, sign-ups, customers, earnings) but no personal data of the people you refer. We store partner-link visits with a one-way hash of the IP address for at most 30 days, only to attribute sign-ups. Details: privacy policy.

8. Ending the partnership

You can leave at any time by e-mail. We can end the program or your participation with 30 days' notice (or right away under section 6). Commission earned on valid payments before the end is still paid out under section 4.

9. Everything else

You act as an independent partner, not as our employee or agent. German law applies. Questions: JarvQuant@gmail.com.
